# Legal launch requirements — River Aftercare

Approved legal copy is **not** in this repository. Do not invent Privacy or Terms text to satisfy SEO or agentic checks.

## About

Public `/about` is published from factual product docs. It does not claim certification, regulatory approval, customer counts, or health outcomes.

## Privacy

**Status:** BLOCKED for launch until counsel-approved copy exists.

Required before production:

- what data clinics submit (enquiry form, staff accounts)
- that patient aftercare pages do not collect patient PII in the MVP
- cookie / theme-preference storage
- subprocessors once production vendors are chosen (Vercel, Neon, Cloudflare, email)
- contact path for privacy requests

Do not publish a placeholder `/privacy` route with unreviewed commitments.

## Terms

**Status:** BLOCKED for launch until counsel-approved copy exists.

Required before production:

- who the customer is (the practice)
- that aftercare copy is clinic-owned
- that the product is not clinical advice or emergency care
- provisional pricing disclaimer until commercial terms are final
- acceptable use / accounts

Do not publish a placeholder `/terms` route with unreviewed commitments.

## Medical / content disclaimer

Patient pages already avoid invented clinical authority. A reviewed disclaimer for clinic-published instructions should be part of the legal pack, not a marketing SEO field.
