# Legal launch requirements — River Aftercare

Approved legal copy is **not** in this repository. Public `/privacy` and `/terms` are **substantial drafts for legal review**. They do not complete the production launch gate.

## About

Public `/about` is published from factual product docs. It does not claim certification, regulatory approval, customer counts, or health outcomes.

## Privacy

**Status:** Substantial draft implemented at `/privacy`. **LEGAL REVIEW STILL REQUIRED.** Not approved.

The published draft covers marketing visitors, contact enquiries, staff accounts, practice configuration, patient-guide architecture (no patient account / no intended patient PII in generic MVP guides), cookies, analytics (none in the current app), service-provider *categories*, hosting/storage (not named as live production vendors), email delivery, security, retention (no invented fixed periods), rights, international processing, children, and contact.

Unresolved before production:

- legal entity name, ABN/ACN, registered address, legal contact email
- named production subprocessors once hosting, database, object storage, DNS, and email are provisioned
- retention schedules
- counsel-approved rights language for the applicable Australian (and any other) privacy regime
- update this policy when analytics or a mail vendor goes live

Do not treat the draft as Privacy Act certification, HIPAA compliance, or a finished subprocessor list.

## Terms

**Status:** Substantial draft implemented at `/terms`. **LEGAL REVIEW STILL REQUIRED.** Not approved.

The published draft covers B2B use, accounts, clinic responsibility for published aftercare, templates (not automatically clinically certified), patient pages as informational, IP and clinic content licence, availability (no uptime guarantee), provisional fees, trial/cancellation principles, disclaimers, cautious liability and indemnity language, and a governing-law **placeholder**.

Unresolved before production:

- legal entity name, ABN/ACN, registered address
- governing State/Territory
- legal contact email
- commercial agreement / subscription terms when billing is final
- counsel review of limitation of liability and indemnity

Do not treat the draft as a finished contract.
